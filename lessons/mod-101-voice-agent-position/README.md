# mod-101-voice-agent-position: The Voice & Multimodal Agent Engineer — Discipline and Position on the Ladder

**Estimated effort:** 10 hours · **Role:** Voice & Multimodal Agent Engineer · **Level:** L35

## What this module is for

Before you build the pipeline, know what you own and what you don't. This module positions the Voice & Multimodal Agent Engineer:

- Against the **six categories of employer** that generate the demand for the role.
- Against the **adjacent roles on the AI Engineering ladder** (L30 feeder roles, L35 peers, L40 upward paths).
- Against the **adjacent voice-ecosystem roles** (ASR / TTS research, conversation / UX design, telephony / contact-centre engineering).
- Against the **deferral contract** — when to own, when to escalate, and what to hand to whom.
- Against the **eight lifecycle deliverables** the role is on the hook for.

Everything the rest of the track builds (pipeline, latency, telephony, safety, eval, orchestration) hangs off the scope you set here.

## Learning objectives

- Characterise the Voice & Multimodal Agent Engineer role as it has emerged at voice-AI pure-plays, realtime-voice orchestration platforms, telephony providers with voice-AI surfaces, frontier labs with realtime voice stacks, applied-voice product companies, and vertical-voice applications.
- Position this role on the AI Engineering level ladder relative to L30 (`applied-ai-engineer`, `agentic-ai-engineer`, `ai-eval-engineer`), L35 (`forward-deployed-engineer`, `ai-infra-inference-engineer`), and L40 (`senior-agentic-ai-engineer`, `ai-engineering-manager`).
- Diagram the relationship to adjacent voice-ecosystem roles (ASR / TTS research, conversation design, voice UX, IVR / CTI, contact-centre ops) and state the sharp boundaries this track enforces.
- Decide the technical-generalist stance — when the voice-agent engineer owns full-stack voice end-to-end vs. when the voice-agent engineer escalates to a peer specialist.
- Name the deliverables the voice-agent engineer is on the hook for at each stage of a voice-feature life cycle — architecture brief, latency budget, speech-pipeline design, telephony integration doc, safety / compliance review, eval bundle, launch-gate decision, operations runbook.
